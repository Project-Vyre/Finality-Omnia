Let's just say they've gotten an appetite + sweet tooth and will no longer work for free. They got jealous of the treatment Blaze Burners have been getting. Hopefully you have some Honey, Chocolate and Sweet Roll, etc. factory schematics in mind.

This also adds Material Influenced Qubits. These need to be sanded (in the case of items) or drained (in the case of fluids) of their Material Data and reused. Material Data then needs to be compacted to produce the duplicated material.
# Item Duplication

The following are required to duplicate solid items.

- `create:honeyed_apple` 1:4x ratio
- `create:sweet_roll`
- `create:chocolate_bar`

# Fluid Duplication

The following are required to duplicate fluids as Molten Metals are no longer bottomless.

- `create:honey` 1:4x ratio
- `create:milk`
- `create:molten_chocolate`
